# Genre render review, 2026-11-22 (the re-read after the serif joints, the G and the italic r changed)

WHAT IT'S FOR: the record behind the dated verdicts in `references/genres.md`, replacing
`docs/reviews/genres-2026-11-19.md` as the one `tests/test_genres.py` holds the recipes to. The 0.10.0 change
(`docs/plan-serif-joint-jags.md`: serif brackets on the stroke's own edge, a level cut on a leaning serif end, the corner
cut along its partner, and a redrawn `G`) moved the outlines of the roman `2 4 5 7 G M Z e z` in every recipe, so every
recipe's outline digest moved, and the rule in `genres.md` section 6 is that a stale digest means a new reading and a new
record, not an edited digest. This record says what was read this time, what was concluded per recipe, and the digests of
the outlines as drawn now.

## What was reviewed

- The page built by `node scripts/concepts/build-page.js --template round1 --concepts templates/concepts.genres.js --content scripts/concepts/content.genres.json`
  with `scripts/gen.js` as 0.10.0 draws it, served with the built neutral Sampler beside it, and shot by `scripts/shoot.py`
  at 1280 px, 2x, `--height 16000 --dump-dom --family Sampler --font-url /fonts/Sampler-VF.woff2`, in headless Chrome 157.
  The DOM reported all ten recipes drawn (`data-fd-rendered="3330"`). The screenshots are not kept in the repository.
- Read by one model reviewer (the implementer, not a fresh reviewer), as in the 2026-11-19 re-read: a targeted reading of
  the letters whose change is measured, not of the genres. The page sets those letters in each recipe's specimen line
  (`ABCDEFGHIJKLM NOPQRSTUVWXYZ abcdefghijklm nopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789 ...`, one line pair per recipe), so every one of the
  nine (`G M Z z 2 4 5 7 e`) was read in all ten recipes from the 2x crops of those lines, at the size the page sets them
  (about 40 px). The roman `G` and `M` of the Didone and of the Clarendon were also enlarged three times. Nothing else
  was re-judged: the italic is not on this page, and nothing outside the nine letters changed, by measurement
  (`tests/test_serif_joints.py::test_only_the_named_glyphs_changed_since_0_9_0`), so the 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-19
  verdicts on contrast, serifs, diagonals and the other letters stand as written.
- What was seen, per recipe: the `G` joins its bowl to the stem in one curve without a step or a corner at the chin; the
  bracketed serifs of `M` and `Z` leave the stem edge without a notch (the serif recipes: didone, fatface, scotch,
  clarendon, reverse, opsz-text, opsz-display); the sans recipes (grot, geometric) show no change beyond the `G`; the
  digits `2 4 5 7` and `z`, `e` show no jag, tooth or fold at their joints. No recipe shows a defect that was not already
  in section 7.
- Limits of the reading: one reviewer, at specimen size with two enlargements, not every glyph at every weight. The
  nine letters were read at Regular, the page's default weight; the Medium and Bold were not read. The 2026-11-16 reading of the lump where the bowls of `P`, `B` and `R` meet the thin stem on `reverse`
  is unchanged and was not re-read.
- The reading changed no verdict.

## Verdicts

| recipe | verdict | date | what was seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| didone | partly | 2026-11-22 | Moderate contrast because of the hairline floor, and equal-weight diagonals on A V W M N v w x y, so it reads as a light modern, not a Didone. The diagonals need an expansion model, which is out of scope |
| fatface | no | 2026-11-22 | Stems are about 1/6 of the cap height even at the Bold; W past its range cannot reach 1/3-cap stems without collapsing the counters. It needs new shapes |
| scotch | reaches | 2026-11-22 | A near-twin of the Didone at these sizes; the bracket and the a terminal are what differ. Also shipped and reviewed as Bract's foil on hopsafari.com (2026-11-15). The italic is refused (section 4) |
| clarendon | no | 2026-11-22 | The serifs are short pointed wedges, not square bracketed slabs, so it reads as a wedge serif. A longer serifLen with serifGrow 0 might fix it; untested. Rendering defects: section 7 |
| slab | reaches | 2026-11-22 | Square unbracketed slabs at stem weight, low contrast, in Rockwell or Memphis proportion. Rendering defects: section 7 |
| grot | partly | 2026-11-22 | A monoline neo-grotesque with level-cut joints and a straight-leg R; the double-storey g with its sharp ear and the hooked a read humanist, which needs skeleton work. Seen after the cut default was fixed (section 7) |
| geometric | no | 2026-11-22 | A wide humanist sans, not Futura-like: it needs a circular O, a single-storey a and pointed apexes, all unbuilt |
| reverse | partly | 2026-11-22 | Reverse contrast, but light, at typewriter weight; a larger W and a lower thin might reach a French Clarendon (untested). Rendering defects, and a lump where the bowls of P, B and R meet the thin stem: section 7 |
| opsz-text | reaches | 2026-11-22 | As a pair: a wider, looser text cut with a taller x-height beside a narrower, higher-contrast display cut. The display cut is close to the Didone |
| opsz-display | reaches | 2026-11-22 | As a pair: a wider, looser text cut with a taller x-height beside a narrower, higher-contrast display cut. The display cut is close to the Didone |

## Outline digests

For each recipe: sha256 over `[mult, key, polygons]` for every key of `G` in sorted order, roman, mult 1, 1.25 then 1.5, each coordinate
rounded to 0.01, as `tests/test_genres.py::current_digests()` computes it, from `scripts/gen.js` as 0.10.0 draws. Recorded
under node major 26 (this machine, node 26.13.1). The node 20 and 22 majors were added on 2026-11-23 from the `genre-digests.json` artifacts
of `record-baseline` run 45191660023 (commit e274764, macOS and Ubuntu, node 20.20.2 and 22.23.5): both machines agreed on each major, and
every digest equals the node 26 one, so the reviewed renders are the outlines those nodes draw. Record any other major the same way and add it
under `by_node_major` (`genre-review-acceptance`). The test recomputes this under the running node and fails with "the genre verdict for <id> is stale" when the digest recorded
for that node major differs, and with instructions to record it when the major is not listed. If it fails after a deliberate change to a
recipe or to `gen.js`, the remedy is a new render review (`genres.md` section 6) and a new record, not editing the digest.
