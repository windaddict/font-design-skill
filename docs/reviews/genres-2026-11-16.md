# Genre render review, 2026-11-16

WHAT IT'S FOR: the record behind the dated verdicts in `references/genres.md`. It says what was reviewed, what the
reviewer concluded per recipe, and the digest of the outlines the generator drew for each recipe at review time, so
`tests/test_genres.py` can fail when a recipe or the generator changes and a verdict no longer describes what is drawn.

## What was reviewed

- The page built by `node scripts/concepts/build-page.js --template round1 --concepts templates/concepts.genres.js --content scripts/concepts/content.genres.json`,
  read at 1280 px, 2x, in headless Chrome, by one fresh strong-model reviewer. One reading, not a panel. The screenshots are not
  kept in the repository.
- The reading found that `grot` and `geometric` drew non-finite coordinates (an unset `cut`). That was fixed in `params()` before
  the verdicts below were written, so those two verdicts are for the fixed generator.
- The digests below were recorded on 2026-11-16, after the review and after the `cut` default, from `scripts/gen.js` as it stood
  then: it had not changed since the review except that default, which made `grot` and `geometric` drawable. Agent edits to
  `gen.js` made after this recording are expected not to change default outlines; the test below is what checks that.

## Verdicts

| recipe | verdict | date | what was seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| didone | partly | 2026-11-16 | Moderate contrast because of the hairline floor, and equal-weight diagonals on A V W M N v w x y, so it reads as a light modern, not a Didone. The diagonals need an expansion model, which is out of scope |
| fatface | no | 2026-11-16 | Stems are about 1/6 of the cap height even at the Bold; W past its range cannot reach 1/3-cap stems without collapsing the counters. It needs new shapes |
| scotch | reaches | 2026-11-16 | A near-twin of the Didone at these sizes; the bracket and the a terminal are what differ. Also shipped and reviewed as Bract's foil on hopsafari.com (2026-11-15). The italic is refused (section 4) |
| clarendon | no | 2026-11-16 | The serifs are short pointed wedges, not square bracketed slabs, so it reads as a wedge serif. A longer serifLen with serifGrow 0 might fix it; untested. Rendering defects: section 7 |
| slab | reaches | 2026-11-16 | Square unbracketed slabs at stem weight, low contrast, in Rockwell or Memphis proportion. Rendering defects: section 7 |
| grot | partly | 2026-11-16 | A monoline neo-grotesque with level-cut joints and a straight-leg R; the double-storey g with its sharp ear and the hooked a read humanist, which needs skeleton work. Seen after the cut default was fixed (section 7) |
| geometric | no | 2026-11-16 | A wide humanist sans, not Futura-like: it needs a circular O, a single-storey a and pointed apexes, all unbuilt |
| reverse | partly | 2026-11-16 | Reverse contrast, but light, at typewriter weight; a larger W and a lower thin might reach a French Clarendon (untested). Rendering defects, including the R: section 7 |
| opsz-text | reaches | 2026-11-16 | As a pair: a wider, looser text cut with a taller x-height beside a narrower, higher-contrast display cut. The display cut is close to the Didone |
| opsz-display | reaches | 2026-11-16 | As a pair: a wider, looser text cut with a taller x-height beside a narrower, higher-contrast display cut. The display cut is close to the Didone |

## Outline digests

For each recipe: sha256 over `[mult, key, polygons]` for every key of `G` in sorted order, roman, mult 1, 1.25 then 1.5, each coordinate
rounded to 0.01. **Mult 1.25 (the Medium) was added after the review**: the reviewed page showed it, but the first record covered only 1 and 1.5.
It was recorded from the generator as it stands now, which has changed since the review only in decision paths, and whose mult 1 and 1.5
digests match the review-time record (checked before the 1.25 digests were added). So the 1.25 digest is not a review-time
observation, and the review-time claim rests on the 1 and 1.5 match. Recorded under node majors 20, 22 and 26 (CI runs 20 and 22; the digests came out identical, because the rounding to 0.01 absorbs V8's arithmetic differences, and they are kept by major anyway so a difference could be recorded). The test recomputes this under the running node and fails with "the genre verdict for <id> is stale" when the digest
recorded for that node major differs, and with instructions to record it when the major is not listed. If it fails after a deliberate change to a recipe or to `gen.js`, the remedy is a new render review (`genres.md` section 6)
and a new record, not editing the digest. To record a new node major, run the digest under it (`npx -y node@<major>` first on PATH, then
`tests/test_genres.py::current_digests()`) after confirming the digests of the recorded majors still match, and add it under `by_node_major`.
